# From Idea to First Scene

This repository is a discussion space for exploring the process of moving from a story idea to a first visual scene using AI-assisted tools.

It exists to support thoughtful, process-level conversation rather than promotion, hype, or finished results.

Most people will arrive here via related videos or writing, but participation is optional and asynchronous.

---

## Context

This space is loosely connected to ongoing work shared at:

- https://www.patreon.com/AIMakingMovies
- https://markdkberry.com  
- https://www.youtube.com/@markdkberry  

Those are points of origin, not authority.

Discussion here is not led, moderated in real-time, or directed toward outcomes.  
The intent is to document thinking, experiments, and limitations as they arise and evolve in a fast changing scene.

---

## Focus of discussion

Topics that belong here include:

- Developing a story or idea toward a *first* scene
- Writing and planning workflows (markdown, scripts, csv use, notes)
- Use of LLMs for planning, critique, or reflection (e.g. ChatGPT, OpenRouter, Local LLMs)
- Managing revision, versioning, and process drift (e.g. Git / Gitlab)
- Storyboarding and visual thinking
- Toolchains that prioritise accessibility and learning

The emphasis is on **process**, not necessarily polished outcomes.

---

## Open source & accessibility

This space leans toward:

- open-source tools  
- low-cost or free workflows
- approaches that are accessible to people without large budgets  

This is not an exclusion of commercial tools or professionals, but an encouragement toward **sharing, comparison, and learning**, rather than gatekeeping or paywalled knowledge.

If a tool, service, or workflow is discussed, it should be in the spirit of explanation, not promotion.

---

## On promotion and selling

Light references to tools, projects, or software that are directly relevant to a discussion are fine.

However, this is **not** a space for:
- sales funnels
- affiliate marketing
- unsolicited pitching
- Patreon or subscription fishing
- promotional posting without substantive contribution

If something is being shared, it should add understanding, not extract attention.

---

## NSFW content

NSFW material is **only appropriate where it is clearly contextualised by narrative, story, or thematic intent**.

This is not a space for:
- pornographic content
- fetish posting
- sexualised imagery without narrative justification

If NSFW material is part of a story or scene being discussed, it should be handled with care and relevance. Assume users may be young.

---

## Participation

- There is no expectation of response from anyone
- Silence is normal
- Incomplete ideas are welcome
- Not every thread will resolve

Browse by category, engage where something resonates, and ignore what doesn’t.

---

## Scope

This repository may evolve as tools, projects, and practices change.

Some discussions may reference specific works or experiments.  
Others may remain abstract or unresolved.

That openness is intentional.
