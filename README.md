# From Idea to First Scene

This repository exists as a discussion space for exploring the process of moving from a story idea to a first visual scene using AI-assisted tools.

It accompanies ongoing videos and experiments, but is not tied to any single project, tool, or methodology.

---

## What this space is for

- Breaking down stories into first scenes
- Exploring writing-first workflows (markdown, scripts, notes)
- Discussing the use of LLMs for planning and critique
- Thinking through versioning and process management
- Developing assets and storyboards
- Understanding where AI helps — and where it fails

The focus is on **process**, not outcomes.

---

## What this space is not

- Not a finished methodology
- Not a support forum
- Not a place for hype or guarantees
- Not a replacement for professional filmmaking

Participation is optional and asynchronous.

---

## How to use this repo

- Use **Discussions** to ask questions or share experiments
- Browse by category rather than timeline
- Engage only where something resonates

Silence is fine. Incomplete ideas are expected.

---

## Scope

This space may evolve as tools and practices change.
Some discussions may reference specific projects or videos.
Others may remain abstract or exploratory.

That flexibility is intentional.
